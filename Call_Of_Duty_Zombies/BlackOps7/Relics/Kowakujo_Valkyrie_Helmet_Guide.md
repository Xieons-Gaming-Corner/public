# Kowakujo — Valkyrie Helmet Relic Guide

## Before starting

- Complete Kowakujo’s main Easter egg at least once on your account.
- Start Cursed Tier 1 or higher and activate Pack-a-Punch.
- Finish in one sitting. Reported bugs include saving/quitting and opening the cat café before rebuilding and collecting the bowl.
- The portal can reportedly open before round 40; round 40 describes trial difficulty rather than a confirmed quest deadline.

## 1. Build the Maneki-Neko tactical

Collect one of each part. Each has three possible spawn locations.

| Part | Possible locations |
|---|---|
| Furin bell | Staging Area: doorway opposite Stamin-Up. Training Area: above the Warden 308 wall-buy toward the upper-right corner. Stables: right side of the collapsed doorway opposite the Arsenal. |
| Karakuri doll | Storage Rooms: table opposite the wall-buy. Storage Rooms: ashes opposite PhD Flopper. Workshop: beside a basket near the windows at the bottom of the stairs. |
| Maneki-Neko statue | Gatehouse: box in the small room right of Speed Cola. Kitchens: barrel opposite Double Tap. Kitchens: corner left of the Mystery Box spawn. |

1. Take all three parts to Workshop.
2. Find the special workbench beside Vulture Aid, not the regular equipment table.
3. Hold interact to craft Maneki-Neko.
4. If consumed, craft another at this bench; the guide lists a 500-Salvage cost.

## 2. Obtain the Nekomancer

### A. Release the caged cat

1. Buy PhD Flopper.
2. Go to Tenshu Entrance near the tree.
3. Face away from Mule Kick and look up for a rooftop cage.
4. Dolphin-dive or wall-jump beneath it so the PhD explosion knocks it down.
5. Pick up the cage.
6. Take it to a lava pool and interact to throw it in.

### B. Follow the fiery paw prints

Get zombie kills near these doorways until red paw prints appear:

- Central Courtyard → Kitchens.
- Kitchens → Flower Garden.
- Staging Area → Stables.
- Training Area doorway south of the Warden 308 wall-buy.

1. Follow the prints’ direction.
2. Get more kills along the path to reveal additional prints.
3. Continue to a glowing paw-print circle.
4. Throw Maneki-Neko into the circle.
5. An Abomination appears and eats the summoned cat.
6. Kill it, targeting its mouths when they glow purple.

### C. Capture the sleeping cat

1. Buy Death Perception.
2. Search Kitchens right of Double Tap, Kitchens near the Mystery Box corner, and inside the Training Area building.
3. Listen for snoring or purring.
4. Use Death Perception to identify squeaky floorboards.
5. Crouch-walk toward the cat without stepping on those boards.
6. Avoid shooting and teammates running across the boards.
7. Hold interact to collect the cat.
8. If scared away, advance a round before retrying.
9. Carry it directly to Pack-a-Punch. Do not drop it or swap weapons; a reported bug can make it disappear.

### D. Transform the cat

1. Place it on the World Seed pedestal beside Pack-a-Punch.
2. Retreat from the damaging pulses.
3. Count five pulses.
4. Approach and melee the seed after the fifth.
5. Retreat and repeat the five-pulse/melee cycle.
6. Collect the Nekomancer when the seed explodes.
7. Pack-a-Punch it.

Hold the aim/alternate-fire input to charge its Hairball attack; the documented PlayStation input is L2.

## 3. Upgrade Maneki-Neko

Craft another basic tactical if the previous step consumed yours.

### A. Matches

1. Go to Kitchens near Double Tap.
2. Find the shelving unit in front of the machine.
3. Check the wooden bucket on the bottom shelf.
4. Collect the book of matches.

### B. Coal

1. Go to the Kitchens room with the Mystery Box spawn.
2. Find the stove in front of that spawn.
3. Go prone in front of it.
4. Hold interact to collect the item.

### C. Gunpowder

1. Activate the Ghostly Riflemen trap in Central Courtyard beside Tenshu Entrance.
2. Move behind the trap toward the south wall.
3. Face the ground near the ghostly riflemen and reload.
4. Collect the white powder that drops.

### D. Apply the upgrade

1. Return to the special Workshop bench beside Vulture Aid.
2. Hold interact to upgrade the tactical.
3. The upgraded tactical creates the bubble needed for bowl shards.

## 4. Collect four bowl shards

1. Gather zombies.
2. Throw upgraded Maneki-Neko.
3. Stand inside its bubble.
4. Charge the Nekomancer Hairball attack.
5. Shoot the ground and let zombies walk into the lingering damage pool.
6. Keep killing while inside the bubble.
7. When the tactical expires or its bubble breaks, inspect its landing area.
8. Collect any tiny, faint-orange shard.
9. Repeat until you have four.

If progress is inconsistent, prioritize lingering-pool kills rather than direct impacts. A fixed kill requirement is not consistently documented; use actual shard drops as your progress check.

## 5. Rebuild and collect the bowl

1. Return to Kitchens.
2. Find the fireplace/fire pit opposite Double Tap, near the sleeping-cat floorboards.
3. Interact to place all four shards.
4. Throw a Molotov onto them.
5. Wait for the bowl to reform.
6. Interact again to pick it up.

Do not finish opening the cat café until this rebuilt bowl is in your possession. You may prepare the mice/cats hunt earlier.

## 6. Complete the cat café

### A. Collect five toy mice

Five mice appear among eight possible locations. Hold interact to collect each.

| Area | Location |
|---|---|
| Gatehouse | Floor at the zombie barrier opposite the wall-buy. |
| Flower Garden | Right corner by the tall shrub, at the barrier between GobbleGum and Mystery Box. |
| Kitchens | Below the weapon rack on the south wall after climbing the Flower Garden stairs. |
| Stables | Inside the zombie hole in the south wall. |
| Training Area | Behind the Ammo Crate beside the wooden pillar. |
| Tenshu Entrance | Collapsed tiles left of the exfil phone booth when facing west. |
| Onsen Baths | Towel on a stool right of Elemental Pop. |
| Central Courtyard | Small lava vein by the large tree, opposite Jugger-Nog. |

### B. Find five cats

| Area | Location |
|---|---|
| Gatehouse | Inside the barrier opposite the wall-buy, on the floor by a basket. |
| Flower Garden | Inside the barrier between GobbleGum and Mystery Box. |
| Kitchens | South wall by Ghostly Riflemen after climbing the Flower Garden stairs. |
| Stables | Ceiling of the building left of Death Perception. |
| Training Area | Wooden beam above the Ammo Box. |
| Tenshu Entrance | Small rooftop west of the exfil phone booth. |
| Onsen Baths | Wooden beam above the stairs to Elemental Pop. |
| Central Courtyard | Barrier behind Jugger-Nog toward the right; jump if needed. |

1. Aim and stare at each cat until it runs away.
2. Updated walkthroughs say staring is sufficient; older guides describe interacting or shooting near it.
3. Advance rounds and check the remaining locations.
4. Cats may not appear immediately after one another; one walkthrough uses every other round.

### C. Open the café and collect catnip

Only finish this interaction after collecting the rebuilt bowl.

1. Go to Tea Garden.
2. Look at the five rooftop cats on the north-side building.
3. Aim/stare until the screen flashes white.
4. Enter the café.
5. Find catnip on a cat tree; one guide found it on the tower facing the entrance.
6. Hold interact to collect it.

## 7. Place the bowl and catnip

1. Go to the large tree in Central Courtyard.
2. Find the flat rock toward the spawn side, in front of the hole in the wall.
3. Interact to place the bowl.
4. Interact again as needed to add catnip.

The bowl can be placed before opening the café. The key ordering requirement is collecting the rebuilt bowl before the café’s final rooftop interaction.

## 8. Feed the bowl during a Courtyard Assault Phase

1. Allow two Assault/Defense phases to fail completely; the walkthrough identifies Flower Garden and Training Area.
2. Let the objectives expire rather than defending them successfully.
3. The next Assault Phase should move to Central Courtyard.
4. During that phase, kill zombies in the Courtyard with charged Nekomancer Hairball pools.
5. Shoot the ground and let enemies walk into the lingering field.
6. Watch for red souls entering the bowl.
7. Continue until Mr. Peeks laughs.

One guide estimates around 50 kills, but the laugh is the reliable completion signal.

## 9. Complete the trial

1. Find the portal inside the Training Area room where the ash pouch is obtained.
2. Enter with upgraded Nekomancer, ammunition, and survival equipment ready.
3. Clear five waves.
4. Use Hairball pools and minimize reloads.
5. Reload only with distance and enough health; reloading causes explosion damage, and reported damage values vary.
6. Complete the final wave to unlock Valkyrie Helmet.

The video walkthrough reports normal zombies on waves 1, 3, and 4, and HVTs on waves 2 and 5—not five exclusively HVT waves.

## Effect when equipped

Areas you linger in begin spawning damaging electric fields.
