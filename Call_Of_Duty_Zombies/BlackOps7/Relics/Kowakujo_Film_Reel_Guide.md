# Kowakujo — Film Reel Relic Guide

## Before starting

- Complete Kowakujo’s main Easter egg at least once on your account.
- Start Cursed Tier 1 or higher.
- Open the map and activate Pack-a-Punch.

## 1. Pack-a-Punch ten unique weapons

1. Keep a useful main weapon for survival.
2. Use another slot for disposable weapons.
3. Buy a weapon you have not counted yet.
4. Pack-a-Punch it once.
5. Write down its name in the checklist below.
6. Replace it with another unique weapon.
7. Pack-a-Punch that weapon once.
8. Repeat until ten different weapons have been upgraded.

### Counting rules

- Pack-a-Punch II and III upgrades do not add unique-weapon credit.
- Another copy of a previously counted gun does not count.
- Pack-a-Punch crystals can count.
- The guide reports a shared team count in co-op: coordinate ten different guns across the squad.
- Use wall-buys for guaranteed choices, then the Mystery Box to fill remaining slots.
- Mule Kick can provide a third slot so you retain two useful weapons while cycling a disposable one.
- Crystals from T.E.D.D. Tasks and Mr. Peeks’ Eggs can reduce Essence costs.

## 2. Prepare for the portal

1. Accumulate Salvage while completing the ten weapons.
2. The walkthrough recommends approximately 15,000 Salvage for scorestreak purchases. This is a preparation target, not a fixed entry requirement.
3. Bring distraction equipment to help manage zombies between scorestreak uses.
4. After the tenth unique weapon upgrade, listen for Mr. Peeks’ laugh.
5. Go to Tea Garden.
6. Find the orange portal against the building’s shelf wall.
7. Interact to enter. In co-op, accept the trial vote.

## 3. Complete the scorestreak-only trial

Repeat for each of the five waves:

1. Read the specific scorestreak requirement on your HUD.
2. Buy that scorestreak at the Crafting Table.
3. Activate it and kill the wave’s zombies.
4. When it runs out, keep moving or use distraction equipment while buying another.
5. Continue until the wave ends.
6. Check the HUD at the start of the next wave; the assigned scorestreak can change.
7. Clear all five waves to unlock Film Reel.

### Important restriction

The trial requires kills from the assigned scorestreak, not simply any streak you carry. Your guns are not the kill solution. Check the HUD before spending Salvage.

## Effect when equipped

You can carry only one Pack-a-Punch weapon at a time.
