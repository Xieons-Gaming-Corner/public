# XGC Relic Emoji Use Policy

XGC uses the relic icons only as small, functional Discord emojis to identify in-game items for community discussion, guides, and gameplay coordination. The purpose is informational and organizational, which is different from the original artwork’s purpose in the game, and the limited size and scope help reduce any market impact.

XGC does not use these emojis as a replacement for the original game assets, does not sell them as merchandise, and does not use them to compete with or substitute for the copyrighted material. The strongest points in favor of fair use are the purpose of the use, the limited amount taken, and the lack of meaningful harm to the original work’s market.

## Copyright Removal Policy

XGC respects the rights of copyright holders. If the owner, authorized agent, or platform operator requests removal of any emoji, image, or other asset used by XGC, XGC will promptly remove the material from all XGC-controlled servers, bots, repositories, and public-facing assets within a reasonable time after receiving notice. This policy applies regardless of whether XGC believes the use may qualify as fair use, and XGC may disable or replace the asset while the request is reviewed.

## Notice and Removal

Upon written request from the copyright owner or authorized representative, XGC will promptly remove or replace the referenced asset from the bot and related community materials. Nothing in XGC’s use of an asset is intended to claim ownership or to override the rights of the original copyright holder.

XGC uses the assets only for community identification and will comply with takedown requests.
